definition and therefore contributes no proof. The proved testing reduction is
more abstract and strictly weaker in content than the publisher's explicit
minimal testing-set theorem. Proposition 3.5 is now represented at its full

excluding-space assertion. This does not by itself prove the integral
maximal-lattice list or either main theorem. No completeness claim is
currently justified.
