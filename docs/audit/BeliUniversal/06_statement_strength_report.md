# Statement strength report

- `IsUniversal` is the exact equality `Q(L)=O`; it is neither weakened to
  unit representation nor strengthened to ambient universality.
- Theorem 2.1 has exactly the paper's integral-lattice hypothesis.  A separate
  theorem proves that its explicit right-hand side implies integrality.
- Rank-dependent coefficient accesses are guarded by actual rank proofs; no
  artificial default invariant weakens a boundary case.
- The revised 2019 condition `(iii')` is used in the unary reduction and is
  proved equivalent to the published Case I/II presentation needed here.
- The unconditional Theorem 3.1 endpoint quantifies over an arbitrary
  prescribed Jordan decomposition and exposes no good-BONG/profile parameter.
- The direct Theorem 3.1 predicate is not a weakening chosen for convenience:
  it is the exact algebraic substitution from the proved Theorem 2.1.  The
  literal printed predicate is also defined, but its theorem has the extra
  hypothesis `fundamentalScaleOrder 0 = 0`.  Claiming the literal theorem
  without this hypothesis would be stronger than the checked derivation.
- Section 4's Lemma 4.1 records source integrality explicitly because
  `IsNUniversal` includes integrality.  The target quantifiers, ranks,
  maximality, and Witt-index conditions are otherwise not restricted.
- Lemma 4.9 concludes the full residual package: order shifts, alpha shifts,
  prefix-product/defect shifts, and prefix-space splitting.  Corollary 4.10
  uses that package rather than a statement-only surrogate.
- Proposition-valued audit structures do not count as proofs; every advertised
  endpoint is inhabited by a theorem checked in the audit module.
