# Paper theorem inventory

The paper develops quadratic-defect preliminaries (Section 1), BONG and
lattice reconstruction (Section 2), binary spinor-norm calculations (Section
3), good BONGs and maximal norm splittings (Section 4), the formula of Theorem
1 (Sections 5–6), and the unit-bounded criteria of Theorems 2–3 (Section 7).

The extracted numbered families are Lemmas 1.1–1.3; Lemmas 2.1–2.3,
Corollaries 2.4, 2.6, Lemma 2.7, Corollary 2.8; the binary results 3.2–3.19;
the good-BONG results 4.1–4.12; Lemmas 6.1–6.7; and Lemmas 7.1–7.3. The three
core theorem cards are:

| Result | Paper objects and assumptions | Conclusion type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theorem 1 | A lattice displayed by a BONG and satisfying property A | Equality describing its integral spinor-norm group |
| Theorem 2 | A lattice with a displayed scaled-hyperbolic tower and residual lattice | Equivalence between unit-bounded spinor norm and explicit tower conditions |
| Theorem 3 | A lattice with a good BONG | Equivalence between unit-bounded spinor norm and explicit adjacent BONG conditions |

The paper assumes throughout a dyadic local field with normalized valuation.
The BONG formulations concern nonempty finite sequences; no rank-zero main
theorem is stated in the audited formulation.
