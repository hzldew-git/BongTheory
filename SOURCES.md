# Mathematical-source boundary

`BongTheory` is the reusable infrastructure layer. It does not claim complete formalization of a particular paper. Paper-specific bibliographic records, exact source hashes, theorem correspondence, and semantic status live in `BeliPapers` and `HePapers`.

Source comments retain the provenance of individual reusable results, including cited results of Beli and O'Meara. Moving a result into this layer does not change its mathematical attribution or turn a conditional interface into an unconditional theorem.
